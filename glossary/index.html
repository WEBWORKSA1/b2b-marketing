Glossary

B2B marketing glossary

65 essential B2B marketing, sales and revenue terms explained in plain English.

ABCDFGILMNOPRSTUVW

A

A/B testing

Comparing two versions of a page, email or ad with randomly split audiences to see which performs better on a defined metric.

Account engagement

A measure of how much activity a target company shows across all its people — visits, content, meetings — rather than per individual lead.

Account-based marketing (ABM)

A strategy where marketing and sales focus coordinated, personalised efforts on a defined list of high-value target accounts.

Activation rate

The share of new users or sign-ups who reach a defined first-value milestone in a product.

Annual contract value (ACV)

The average yearly revenue from a customer contract, excluding one-time fees.

Attribution

Assigning credit for conversions or revenue to the marketing touches that preceded them.

Average deal size

Total closed-won revenue divided by the number of deals in a period.

B

Buyer enablement

Content and tools that help buyers complete their own internal buying tasks, such as building a business case.

Buyer persona

A semi-fictional profile of a typical buyer role, describing goals, pains, objections and information sources.

Buying committee

The group of people — often five or more — who influence or approve a B2B purchase.

C

Churn rate

The share of customers or revenue lost over a period.

Closed-lost / closed-won

CRM stages marking deals that were lost or won.

Cold outreach

Unsolicited one-to-one prospecting by email, phone or social to people with no prior relationship.

Content syndication

Distributing your content through third-party publishers, often in exchange for leads.

Conversion rate

The percentage of people who take a desired action out of those who had the opportunity.

Cost per lead (CPL)

Program spend divided by leads generated.

Cost per opportunity

Program spend divided by sales opportunities created — a better efficiency metric than CPL in B2B.

CRM

Customer relationship management software that stores contacts, accounts, deals and activity.

Customer acquisition cost (CAC)

Total sales and marketing cost to win new customers, divided by the number of new customers.

Customer lifetime value (LTV)

Expected gross profit from a customer over the whole relationship.

D

Dark social

Sharing that happens in private channels (messages, private communities) that analytics cannot track.

Data enrichment

Adding firmographic, technographic or contact details to existing records from external sources.

Demand capture

Marketing aimed at buyers already in-market, such as search ads and review sites.

Demand creation

Marketing that builds awareness and preference among buyers who are not yet actively buying.

Demand generation

The full set of activities that create and capture interest in your offering and turn it into pipeline.

F

Firmographics

Company attributes used for targeting: industry, size, revenue, location, ownership.

Funnel

A model of the stages a buyer passes through from awareness to purchase.

G

Gated content

Content that requires a form submission to access.

Go-to-market (GTM)

The plan for how a company reaches target customers and achieves competitive advantage.

I

Ideal customer profile (ICP)

A description of the type of company that gets the most value from your product and is most valuable to you.

Inbound marketing

Attracting buyers with useful content and experiences so they come to you.

Intent data

Signals that a company or person is researching a topic, product category or competitor.

L

Lead

A person who has shared contact details or been identified as a potential buyer.

Lead magnet

A valuable resource offered in exchange for contact details.

Lead nurturing

Ongoing, relevant communication that helps leads progress toward buying.

Lead scoring

Assigning points to leads based on fit and behaviour to prioritise follow-up.

Lifecycle stage

A lead's position in the buying journey, e.g. subscriber, lead, MQL, SQL, opportunity, customer.

Lookalike audience

An ad audience built from people similar to an existing customer or lead list.

LTV:CAC ratio

Lifetime value divided by acquisition cost; a common gauge of acquisition efficiency.

M

Marketing automation

Software that automates email, scoring, segmentation and workflows.

Marketing mix modelling (MMM)

Statistical analysis estimating each channel's contribution to outcomes from aggregate data.

Marketing-qualified lead (MQL)

A lead that meets agreed fit and engagement criteria and is ready for sales review.

Martech

Marketing technology — the software stack marketers use.

N

Net revenue retention (NRR)

Revenue from an existing customer group after expansion, contraction and churn, as a share of starting revenue.

O

Opportunity

A qualified potential deal tracked in the CRM with an expected value and close date.

P

Payback period

Months needed for gross profit from a customer to repay the cost of acquiring them.

Pipeline

The total value of open sales opportunities.

Pipeline velocity

How fast revenue moves through pipeline: opportunities × win rate × deal size ÷ cycle length.

Positioning

How you define your product's category, audience and differentiation in buyers' minds.

Product-led growth (PLG)

A strategy where the product itself drives acquisition, conversion and expansion.

Product-qualified lead (PQL)

A user or account whose in-product behaviour signals buying readiness.

Programmatic advertising

Automated buying of digital ad inventory, often with account or audience targeting.

R

Retargeting

Showing ads to people who previously visited your site or engaged with your content.

Revenue operations (RevOps)

The function aligning systems, data and processes across marketing, sales and customer success.

S

Sales cycle

Time from first qualified contact to closed deal.

Sales enablement

Content, training and tools that help sales teams sell more effectively.

Sales-qualified lead (SQL)

A lead accepted by sales as worth pursuing as a potential opportunity.

Service-level agreement (SLA)

A written agreement between marketing and sales on lead volume and follow-up commitments.

Share of voice

Your brand's portion of conversation or visibility in a market versus competitors.

T

Technographics

Data on which technologies a company uses.

Thought leadership

Expert, original perspectives that build authority and trust.

Total addressable market (TAM)

The total revenue opportunity if you won every possible customer.

U

UTM parameters

Tags added to URLs (source, medium, campaign) so analytics can attribute visits.

V

Value proposition

A concise statement of the benefit you deliver and why it matters to a specific buyer.

W

Win rate

Closed-won opportunities divided by all closed opportunities.
