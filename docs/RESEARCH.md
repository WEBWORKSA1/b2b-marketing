## Synthesis
1. Free interactive **calculators/tools** + **long guides** = the traffic and AdSense engine (Martech Zone, Saffron Edge, Databox, HubSpot).
2. The highest-value conversion is a **named free audit / expert match** (Directive, Whitehat, Zoftware) → sell qualified leads to agencies/vendors.
3. Side revenue: **sponsored listings, media kit, donations, newsletter sponsorship, job posts, contest sponsorship** (FeedSpot, Martech Zone, SalesTechStar).
4. Trust: research-style content, FAQ blocks, key-takeaway boxes, comparison tables, transparent disclosures.
